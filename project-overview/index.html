Architecture overview

  • Frontend: learner experience, dashboards, course player.
  • Content layer: course schema, modules, lessons, pages, interactions.
  • API layer: course fetch, progress save, assessment submit, admin actions.
  • Security: auth, RBAC, tenant isolation, audit logs.
  • Data: course metadata, attempts, results, completions, certificates.
  • Media: narration, transcripts, videos, downloadable assets.

Backend service architecture

Course Service

Handles course metadata, modules, lesson definitions, page content, and versioning policy.

Assignment Service

Assigns mandatory training to employees, teams, departments, or roles and tracks deadlines.

Progress Service

Stores page completion, resume state, lesson state, and interaction progress for each learner.

Assessment Service

Validates answers, scores attempts, enforces retry logic, and records pass/fail outcomes.

Certificate Service

Checks completion rules and issues, renews, or revokes certificates with audit logging.

Audit Service

Captures privileged actions, publishing events, grading, and evidence-related changes.

Prototype scope

  • One mandatory AI training course for all employees.
  • One mandatory AI training course with six content modules.
  • Four sections and a knowledge check in every module.
  • Animated four-card content interaction in Module 1.
  • Final assessment, local completion state and narration controls.
  • Admin and server-side capabilities remain future prototype work.

Key notes

  • This is a prototype-first approach, not the final enterprise LMS.
  • We are validating experience, structure, and reusability before broad admin expansion.
  • Security and assessment integrity remain server-side responsibilities.
  • Future phases will add reporting, certificates, RBAC, and enterprise rollout features.
